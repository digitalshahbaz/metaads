## Patterns
1. **"Install your AI marketing team" is now a category.** UnicornCoach (94 ads, running since August) uses almost the same line as your "Your Own AI Marketing Team — ₹999".
2. **₹97–₹299 AI kits used as tripwires:** Instant AI Funnel Builder Kit (₹97), Orygin AI (₹299, "offer valid until midnight"). They sell templates and agents, not courses.
3. **"Funnel doctor" hooks work for coaches:** Harmeet Kelley's "Your Funnel Is Leaving Money Behind / Find The Hidden Leak". These are diagnosis-first hooks that lead to a call.
4. **Anti-funnel positioning:** CoachEasily "Skip the Funnel", Miles Stutz "The Book-a-Call Model Is Dying". Contrarian hooks get attention.
5. **Low-ticket to high-ticket without sales calls:** Miles Stutz has run "$17 buyers become high-ticket clients in 7 days" for about 2 months. It's the same model as your ₹999 bundle, where the ₹999 buyer becomes a back-end client.
6. **WhatsApp + AI automation funnels** are a growing India-specific niche (AI Autofunnel, Ai-preneur, Navin Parmar).
