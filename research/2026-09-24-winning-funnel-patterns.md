# Winning funnel patterns: Meta-ads digital products (Ad Library deep dive)

_24 Sep 2026. Sources: Meta Ad Library via the connector (titles, pages, start dates, active counts) and web search for offers and prices._

**Limits of this research:**
- The connector returns no ad copy and no destination URLs. I couldn't open the landing pages from this environment because egress to those domains is blocked.
- Funnel steps come from each advertiser's public pages and press coverage.
- "Winner" means the advertiser has kept ads running for a long time, or keeps many ads live. Nobody spends for 60–120+ days on a loser.

## How to spot a winner in the Ad Library
1. **Long run time.** An ad that has been live for 60+ days is profitable. MR.Digital has one ad live since ~21 May (about 125 days).
2. **Many duplicates of the same title.** This means the advertiser is scaling by duplication. SVIZ Academy runs 8 active copies of "Create Viral Content with FREE AI Tools ₹349". Brandon Willington and Where U? run 364 ads with the same core hook.
3. **Niche variants of one offer.** Where U? runs "…for Coaches" and "…for Contractors". Neil Shoney claims wins "in 21+ different niches". This means the core offer works and they are widening the audience.

## The 6 winning patterns

### Pattern 1: Evergreen "system install" → application → high ticket (Neil Shoney)
- **Where to see it:** [Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=ALL&view_all_page_id=100805602791691). 70 active ads, some live since mid-June (~100 days).
- **Hooks (titles):**
  - Social proof by count: "2,712+ Coaches Have Installed This Ads System"
  - Outcome: "6-Figure Months In 21+ Different Niches"
  - Differentiation: "Ads That Scale (Because They're Unique to You)"
- **Funnel:**
  - Ad → free training ("The Shoney 2-5-5 Method") → application/call.
  - Back end: [90 Day Ads & Funnels Mastermind](https://adsandfunnels.com/), with 3 live group calls a week and 36 frameworks.
- **Why it works:**
  - A **named method** (2-5-5) makes it sound proprietary.
  - The student counter is a number that grows over time.
  - "Install a system", not "learn a course".
  - The front end is free, and the money is in the back end.

### Pattern 2: "Fire your agency" DIY workshop plus support (Brandon Willington / Where U?, AU)
- **Where to see it:**
  - [Brandon's Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=ALL&view_all_page_id=401619216906924)
  - [Where U?'s Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=ALL&view_all_page_id=110749043642049)
  - 364 active ads between them. This is the biggest scaler in the Meta-ads education niche.
- **Hooks:**
  - "Fire Your Agency. Learn to Run Your Ads."
  - "Leave With Your Ads On"
  - "You Don't Need a Marketing Degree"
  - "2 Days Building Your Ads. 6 Months of Support."
  - Testimonial titles such as "He 10x'd His Business…"
- **Funnel:**
  - Ad → workshop booking page → 2-day workshop (in person or online).
  - The workshop comes with **6 months of support, with several daily advisory Zoom calls** ([Canberra Times](https://www.canberratimes.com.au/story/9183158/how-brandon-willington-turned-attention-into-a-national-workshop-movement/)).
- **Why it works:**
  - **The enemy is the agency.**
  - The promise is concrete: "you leave with your ads live".
  - Support time is the value stack.
  - The founder speaks unfiltered, like a creator, not a corporation.

### Pattern 3: Community subscription with a face (Ben Heath, Roota Mittal)
- **Where to see it:**
  - Ben Heath's page 192870247712035, live since ~30 Jul.
  - Roota's offer: skool.com/roota at $7/month.
- **Funnel:** Ad → Skool "about" page → monthly membership.
  - [Ben Heath Meta Ads Academy](https://www.skool.com/benheath/about): £97/month, weekly live Q&A, direct access to Ben.
- **Why it works:**
  - Recurring revenue.
  - "Direct access to <name>" beats the content itself.
  - Skool's social proof (member count, feed) does the selling.

### Pattern 4: AI or Claude "run ads without Ads Manager" course (Webprofits, Sanskar Tiwari)
- **Webprofits:** [webprofitsacademy.com.au/claude](https://webprofitsacademy.com.au/claude/). 117 active ads.
  - Offer: $599 one-time, or inside the membership.
  - Contents: 6 hours, 14 modules, a starter **skills library**, live demos of Meta campaigns built in real time, and "no coding needed".
  - The upsell is the full membership: daily coaching, frameworks and community.
- **Sanskar Tiwari:** [sanskartiwari.io](https://www.sanskartiwari.io/). "Run Meta ads without knowing ads" from ₹199.
  - Contents: 12-chapter video course of real ads run with Claude Code, plus a PDF playbook and a prompt pack.
- **Why it works:**
  - The new-mechanism story: "AI does the work; you don't need to learn Ads Manager".
  - Screen-recorded proof of the AI running real campaigns.
  - **This is the same angle as your AMAB bundle.** It's already proven worldwide, and in India only Sanskar is clearly running it.

### Pattern 6: Lead magnet PDF → call (Aleric Heck / AdOutreach)
- **Where to see it:** 63 active ads. Landing page: go.adoutreach.com/pdf-gift-fb.
- **Funnel:**
  - Ad → free "$200M+ Strategy" PDF (19 pages) → email nurture → "free strategy session" call → done-for-you or done-with-you service ([strategy session](https://adoutreach.com/strategy-session/)).
- **Why it works:** A big-number authority claim in the lead magnet's name, and the PDF pre-sells the call.

## The recurring blueprint (common to all 6)
1. **A named mechanism:** 2-5-5 Method, "Ads System", "Claude Code for marketers".
2. **An enemy:** agencies, expensive ₹5,000 courses, Ads Manager complexity.
3. **A concrete finish line:** "leave with your ads on", "install the system", "your laptop + AI = team".
4. **Proof with a counter:** "2,712+ coaches", "He 10x'd…". The counter goes up every month.
5. **Live access or support as the main value:** 6 months of support, weekly Q&A, daily calls. Nobody leads with "X hours of videos".
6. **The back end is where the money is:** mastermind, membership or done-for-you. The front end only has to break even.
7. **Scale by duplication and niche variants,** not by editing the winner.
