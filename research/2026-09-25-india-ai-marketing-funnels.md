## Funnel types seen
1. **₹9 or free AI workshop → high-ticket program** (Be10x, Outskill, Navin Parmar, Institute of Digital Skills). This model has the biggest scale in India, with 200–400 ads per page. The workshop is only a lead magnet.
2. **₹99 live workshop** (Jaysanaifilm, FITA "AI Agent ₹99"). The upsell happens on the live session.
3. **₹999 course or live session** (Rohith AI Marketer, DigitalJahid, you). Rohith has run this for about 2 months with urgency ("₹999 today, ₹14,999 tomorrow") and proof ("3000+ enrolled").
4. **Guarantee-led live workshop** (Connectandcollabs: "Create your first AI video live or get 110% money back").
5. **Claude-specific** (AI Film Makers "Run Better Meta Ads With Claude + ChatGPT", Sanskar, Navin, Roota). Claude is now a mainstream hook in India. Being first to it no longer sets you apart; proof and a named system will.
