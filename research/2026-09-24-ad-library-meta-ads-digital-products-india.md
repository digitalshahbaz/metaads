## What this means for Digital Shahbaz (₹999 AI Meta Ads Bundle)
1. **Price position:** most Meta-ads info products in India sit at **₹49–₹499**. At ₹999 you're a premium front end, so the ad has to justify ₹999 against ₹199 AI ebooks. The live coaching, community and "AI does the work" are the difference. A ₹300 "Meta Ads Bundle" competitor exists, so lead with *what's unique*, not "bundle".
2. **Borrow the hot hooks:** test ads on "Andromeda update 2026" and "ChatGPT/Claude + Meta Ads" angles. The Andromeda-bundle offer (Swift vault) is the longest-running ad seen.
3. **Tripwire caution:** your own data says ₹99/₹199 tripwires lost money without an upsell (ROAS 0.17–0.41). If you test a ₹199–₹249 "AI Meta Ads Playbook" front end, the ₹999 bundle must be the order bump or immediate upsell, judged on combined order value.
4. **Niche playbooks** (real estate, interiors, clinics, coaches) are a gap. Only a few advertisers go niche-specific. One page per niche, same core product.
5. **Watch next:**
   - Swift vault (58 d)
   - ProdxStore (30 d)
   - The Ai Advertising OS (17 d)
   - Shubh marketing (15 d)
   - Doersdigilab (heavy new launch)

   Any of these still running in 2–3 weeks is almost certainly profitable. Open their ads in the Ad Library to study the funnels.

## Global patterns
1. **Free PDF/playbook → call or high ticket** is the dominant model at scale: Hormozi, Aleric Heck, Ezra, Confect, Jenna Elizabeth.
2. **Niche Meta-ads education runs longest:** Dippidi (real estate, 114 days), nPhoto Lab (photographers), "Meta Ads System for Experienced Coaches", The Trades Marketer, Nicole Eldridge (local businesses).
3. **The same three hooks show up worldwide:**
   - "Andromeda changed Meta ads": CHAMBER, Confect, Swift vault, Doersdigilab
   - "ChatGPT Ads": Ezra, several in India
   - "Claude/AI runs your ads": Webprofits $599, Sanskar Tiwari, Roota, Zeely
4. **Community pricing to compare against** the ₹999 + 12-month coaching bundle:
   - Dippidi: $39/month
