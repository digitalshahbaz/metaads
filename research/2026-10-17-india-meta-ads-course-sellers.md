## How this was collected
- **Source:** the Meta Ad Library connector, India, active ads only.
  - facebook.com itself is blocked in this cloud environment, so a browser could not be used.
  - The connector returns page, ad headline, ad count and dates. It does **not** return the landing-page URL or body copy. Open each page's Ad Library link and click an ad to see its landing page.

## Takeaways
1. **Survivors keep their winners running.** Digital Hrithik (~6 months) and MR.Digital (~5 months) are the longest-running Meta-ads course sellers, and neither has touched its winning ads in months.
2. **₹999 is a crowded price point.**
   - Direct competitors: Rohith (71 ads, Malayalam) and Digital Marketer Land.
   - Below you: many ₹49–₹499 tripwires.
3. **Claude + Meta ads is the new hook.** Anish Jajodia's "Live Meta Ads Audit with Claude" is the fastest-growing new entrant (139 ads, launched 16 Oct). AI Film Makers and Akhil Namdev have dropped the angle.
4. **Regional language is under-served.** Malayalam (Rohith), Tamil (Abilesh), Marathi (Meta Reach 07). There is still no large Hindi Claude + Meta ads seller apart from you.
