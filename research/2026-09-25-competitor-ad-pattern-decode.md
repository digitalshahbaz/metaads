## Cross-advertiser patterns (what's working)
1. **Duplicate, don't edit.**
   - Every winner runs 5–200 copies of the same headline.
   - They add fresh copies in batches every 1–3 days (Adnomics, UnicornCoach) or every month (Digital Hrithik).
2. **An enemy or contrast in the headline:**
   - "Stop X. Start Y", "Fire Your Agency", "The Old Way Is Guilty"
   - "Your dashboard is lying", "Stop Paying ₹5,000"
3. **A named mechanism or unique angle:** "Maths Behind Meta Ads", "AI Marketing Team", "Business OS", "Inside Build With Roota".
4. **Time-boxed, LIVE promises:** "in 3 Hours", "2-Day LIVE", "in 2 Evenings".
5. **Stacked skills with Claude:** "WhatsApp + Claude AI", "Claude + ChatGPT", "Claude + 25 AI Tools". Claude is now mainstream.
6. **Price in the headline, and price tests:** UnicornCoach ₹99 against FREE, Roota "$37/mo", MR.Digital "₹5,000".
7. **Regional-language and niche variants:** AI Film Makers in Tamil and Gujarati, UnicornCoach for trading coaches.
