# LinkedIn content brief: patterns from the Meta Ad Library (for the Digital Shahbaz team)

**What this is based on:** 5 rounds of Meta Ad Library research (24–26 Sep 2026) covering about 60 Indian and global advertisers who sell Meta ads, AI marketing and digital-product courses. The patterns below come from ads that have run for weeks or months and been copied many times over. Advertisers only keep paying for ads that make money, so these are proven.

**Rule:** borrow the *pattern*, never the exact line. Every post should be in Shahbaz's voice, backed by our own proof: ₹30Cr+ ad spend managed, our real account numbers, and the ₹830 break-even maths.

---

## 1. The 8 winning patterns, turned into LinkedIn content

### Pattern 1: Name an enemy
**Seen in:** UnicornCoach "Fire Your Marketing Agency", "The Old Way Is Guilty"; MR.Digital "Stop Paying ₹5,000 Per Course".
**Why it works:** readers who share the frustration nod along, and a named villain makes the post stick.
**How to use it on LinkedIn:** open every post against something: agencies, guesswork, boosting posts, gurus, "just increase the budget", vanity metrics.
- "Your agency isn't lying to you. They just don't know either."
- "Stop boosting posts. Here's what that ₹500 actually buys you."

### Pattern 2: "Stop X. Start Y"
**Seen in:** UnicornCoach "Stop Chasing Leads. Get Buyers", "Stop Consuming. Start Building"; Ai-preneur "Stop Doing Manually What AI Can Automate".
**How to use it on LinkedIn:** as the first line, or as a carousel cover.
- "Stop chasing cheap leads. Start tracking cost per *sale*."
- "Stop writing ad copy from scratch. Start with a Claude prompt system."

### Pattern 3: A contrarian truth (the curiosity gap)
**Seen in:** Sannidhya Baweja "Your dashboard is lying" (20 copies), the best single hook in the research; Miles Stutz "The Book-a-Call Model Is Dying"; CoachEasily "Skip the Funnel".
**How to use it on LinkedIn:** state the uncomfortable fact, then prove it with our own data.
- "Meta showed 26 purchases. The bank showed 1. Here's what happened." (our real 18 Aug case)
- "A 4% CTR ad lost me money. A 1.8% CTR ad made it. Here's why."

### Pattern 4: Numbers as the method ("the maths behind…")
**Seen in:** Adnomics Academy "Learn The Maths Behind Meta Ads" on all 23 ads.
**How to use it on LinkedIn:** teach one number per post. Numbers build trust with business owners and look like expertise.
- "The ₹830 rule: the only number I check before scaling any ad."
- "Break-even ROAS = price ÷ max cost per sale. For a ₹999 product it's 1.2. Most people don't know theirs."

### Pattern 5: Time-boxed promises
**Seen in:** AI Film Makers "Master Claude + 25 AI Tools in 3 Hours", "Build Your First AI Agent in Under 3 Hours"; Dominators "Launch Your 1st Digital Product in 2 Evenings"; AyLence "in 1 Day".
**How to use it on LinkedIn:** give a concrete result with a time frame. Keep it believable.
- "I built a full ad campaign with Claude in 40 minutes. Here's every prompt."
- "3 AI tools that save me 10 hours a week on ad reporting."

### Pattern 6: Stacked skills with AI ("X + Claude")
**Seen in:** Navin Parmar "WhatsApp Marketing + Claude AI" (about 200 copies); AI Film Makers "Meta Ads With Claude + ChatGPT"; Roota "Market Better With Claude".
**How to use it on LinkedIn:** always pair AI with a business skill. Claude is now a mainstream word in India; alone it's no longer special, but paired with a job ("Meta ads + Claude") it is.
- "Meta Ads + Claude: my exact 5-step workflow."
- "WhatsApp follow-up + AI: how we recover abandoned checkouts."

### Pattern 7: Show one piece of the system per post
**Seen in:** Roota Mittal: every ad sells one module ("Profitable Niches", "High-Converting VSLs", "Market Better With Claude"), all ending "…Inside Build With Roota".
**How to use it on LinkedIn:** make each post one lesson from the bundle, with a soft mention of where the rest lives.
- "One prompt from our Claude Marketer Kit (the full kit is in the AI Meta Ads Bundle)."
- A different piece each week: ad concepts, the audit checklist, the reporting template, the scaling rules.

### Pattern 8: Identity and "after office hours"
**Seen in:** Digital Hrithik "Build Your Own Business. After Office Hours" (running since April); UnicornCoach "You Don't Need Millions of Followers to Scale".
**How to use it on LinkedIn:** speak to *who the reader wants to become*: a solo founder, a coach who runs their own ads, a marketer who isn't replaceable.
- "You don't need an agency. You need a system and 30 minutes a day."
- "The marketers who survive AI won't be the ones who ignore it."

## 2. Word bank

**Power words (use these):**
- *Verbs:* Stop, Start, Fire, Install, Build, Launch, Scale, Crack, Decode, Master, Automate
- *Contrast:* Old way / New way, Manual vs AI, Guessing vs Knowing, Leads vs Buyers
- *Proof and numbers:* ₹30Cr+ managed, "exact framework", "my real numbers", "₹830 rule", "break-even", "cost per sale"
- *Tools:* Claude, ChatGPT, AI Marketing Team, Business OS, System, Playbook, Prompt Kit
- *Time:* "in 40 minutes", "in 1 day", "in 3 hours", "this week"
- *Curiosity:* "Your dashboard is lying", "Nobody tells you this", "The maths behind", "What happened next"
- *Hinglish (works in India):* "Bhai", "Kya aap bhi…", "Seedhi baat"

**Avoid (overused or generic):**
- "Boost / Maximize / Unlock / Supercharge your results" (filler that some advertisers had AI write for them; nobody remembers it)
- "Game-changer", "revolutionary", "secret sauce"
- Income claims ("₹1Cr/year"). They look scammy on LinkedIn and risk Meta policy problems if reused in ads.

## 3. Six LinkedIn post templates

1. **Dashboard-lying story**
   - Hook: "Meta said X. Reality said Y."
   - Body: what went wrong (tracking), how we found it, the fix.
   - Close with a lesson and a question to the reader.
2. **The maths post**
   - Hook: "The only number I check before scaling: ₹___."
   - Body: the formula, a worked example, the mistake most people make.
3. **Stop X / Start Y carousel**
   - Cover: "Stop ___. Start ___."
   - Then 5 slides, each with one old habit and its replacement.
4. **AI workflow reveal**
   - Hook: "I did ___ in ___ minutes with Claude."
   - Body: steps 1–5 plus one prompt shared in full.
   - Close: "Want the full kit? Link in the comments."
5. **Enemy post**
   - Hook: "Why I'd fire most agencies."
   - Body: 3 specific failures, what to do instead, and why it's our method.
6. **Module spotlight (weekly)**
   - Hook: one result from one piece of the bundle.
   - Body: show the piece (a screenshot of the template or prompt).
   - Close: "This is 1 of 10 tools inside the AI Meta Ads Bundle."

## 4. Posting rules taken from how the ads run
- **Repeat what wins.** Top advertisers run the same headline 20–200 times. If a post does well, repost the idea in a new format (text, then carousel, then video) 2–4 weeks later.
- **Refresh every 3–5 weeks.** Digital Hrithik has kept one offer alive since April by re-launching it monthly. Keep 3–4 core messages and rotate their formats.
- **Test in batches.** Post 5–6 different hooks across 2 weeks, see which gets saves and DMs (not just likes), then do more of that one.
- **Send the best posts to the ads team.** A LinkedIn post that gets strong engagement is a candidate hook for a Meta ad.
