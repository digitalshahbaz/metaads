## 2. The 18 Aug spike: check your real sales first

On **18 Aug** Meta recorded **26 purchases from ₹2,021 spend, 31 LPVs and 1 checkout started** (ROAS 12.9). Credited to MTP02 (14), Ad-2 (7) and Ad-3 (2). Each purchase is worth about ₹999, so these were full-price sales.

26 people can't buy from 31 page visits and 1 checkout. Most likely you sent a **WhatsApp, email or live broadcast** that day, buyers came through that link, and Meta credited them to ads they had seen or clicked earlier. The other possibility is the purchase event firing twice. **Either way, the ads didn't drive those sales.**

Adjusting for it (keeping 1 real purchase that day, removing 25 × ₹999):

| | As reported | Adjusted |
|---|---|---|
| Phase A (2–21 Aug) purchases | 90 | **65** |
| Phase A cost/purchase | ₹476 | **₹659** |
| Phase A ROAS | 2.31 | **1.73** |
| MTP02 "most practical" | 51 @ ₹478, ROAS 2.30 | **37 @ ₹659, ROAS 1.72** |
| Ad-2 (08 Aug) | 15 @ ₹416, ROAS 2.40 | 8 @ ₹780, ROAS 1.28 |

**Question for you:** what happened on 18 Aug (a broadcast, a live session, a launch email)? And did Razorpay show about 26 sales that day?

## 3. What actually changed after 22 Aug (adjusted numbers)

| Funnel step | Phase A: 2–21 Aug (old setup) | Phase B: 22 Aug → now (New LP era) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spend | ₹42,805 (≈₹2.1k/day) | ₹92,232 (≈₹2.8k/day) | +33%/day |
| Cost per landing page view | ₹32.7 | ₹35.9 | +10% |
| **LPV → checkout started** | **7.3%** | **5.3%** | **−27%** |
| Checkout started → purchase | 68% | 67% | No change |
| LPV → purchase | 5.0% | 3.5% | −30% |
| **Cost per purchase** | **₹659** | **₹1,014** | **+54%** |
| **ROAS** | **1.73** | **0.97** | |

**How to read this:**
1. **The ads aren't the main problem.** Traffic costs only 10% more.
2. **Checkout and payment aren't the problem.** 2 in 3 people who start checkout still pay, the same as before.
3. **The problem is the landing page, before the "Buy" click.** 27% fewer visitors decide to start checkout. That points to the page or offer (headline, how the bundle is presented, price framing, "founding member / 12-month coaching" story, page speed) since the New LP went live around 22–24 Aug.
4. A smaller factor: daily spend went up about 33% at the same time, and higher spend pushes cost per purchase up for a while.

**What would prove this wrong:** send the MTP02 creative to the old page and to the new page side by side. If both convert at similar rates (LPV→checkout within ±1 point), the page isn't the cause and it's audience fatigue or seasonality.

### Creative patterns
1. **Practical "how I actually do it" hooks win.** MTP02, MTP05 "Claude can run" and Ads_Campaign_Structure beat value-stack and "launch" ads. The Value-Stack test got ₹1.5k → 1 purchase. "Launch" ads ran at ₹2,282 per purchase.
2. **Real people on screen with the Meta dashboard works** (AiVid 2 at ₹590/purchase). "Bhai 5 naye client" income-claim style didn't convert (0 on ₹1.7k).
3. **A high click-through rate doesn't mean sales.** MTP03 (8.8% CTR) and MTP01 (9.0%) got cheap clicks, and V-2 got clicks at 1.9% LPV→checkout. Curiosity hooks bring browsers. Judge ads on LPV→checkout and cost per purchase, not CTR.
4. **Videos and statics come out about the same.** Videos: ₹40k spend at ₹825/purchase. Statics: ₹95k at ₹717/purchase (static is inflated by the 18 Aug spike). Keep both formats.
5. **Duplicating a winner doesn't bring its results back.** "V-1 – Copy" got ₹1,188 → 1 purchase after the original (₹810/purchase) was archived. "AiVid 2 – Copy" got ₹952 → 0. The learning stays with the original ad ID. **Don't archive or duplicate winners. Leave them running.**

## 5. How the account was run: what cost money

| Pattern | Evidence | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Pausing winners** | Training 02/08 (₹466/purchase) paused ≈21 Aug. AMTP 6 Aug (ROAS 4.3) paused after ₹1.4k. 08 Aug and 12 Aug (ROAS 2.1–2.2) paused. | Replaced by setups at ₹1,000+ per purchase |

| **New campaigns instead of new ad sets** | 9 bundle campaigns in 7 weeks. Most ran under 10 days. | Meta keeps restarting its learning from scratch |
| **Too many ads for the budget** | 20 creatives at ₹2,000/day (≈₹100 each, floor is ₹300–500). 39 ads under ₹500. | Most ads never tested |

| **Ignoring fatigue** | Hanish ad set frequency 8.1. Cost/purchase went from ₹748 lifetime to ₹1,083 in the last 7 days. | Rising cost per purchase |
