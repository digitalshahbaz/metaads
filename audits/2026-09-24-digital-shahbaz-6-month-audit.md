## 2. The main finding: the ₹999 funnel broke around 22 August

Looking only at the ₹999 sales campaigns:

| Phase | Campaigns | Spend | Purchases | Cost / purchase | ROAS | Cost / LPV | LPV → purchase |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A: 2–21 Aug** | AI Meta Ads Training 02/08, DS META 999 08 Aug, 12 Aug, AMTP 6 Aug | ₹42,805 | 90 | **₹476** | **≈ 2.3** | ₹32.7 | **6.9%** |
| **B: 22 Aug → now** | Bundle 22/08, **Bundle 24 Aug "New LP"**, ChatGPT 199, Value-Stack test, AMAB999 20-Creative, Testing Camp | ₹96,640 | 100 | **₹966** | **≈ 0.95** | ₹36.1 | **3.7%** |

- The cost of getting someone to the page barely changed (+10% cost per landing page view).
- The share of page visitors who buy **almost halved** (6.9% → 3.7%).
- So the problem is **after the click**, on the landing page or in the offer. It isn't targeting or the ads. The timing matches the switch to "New LP" and the repositioning from "AI Meta Ads Training" to "AI Meta Ads Bundle / founding members / 12-month coaching".
- The Phase A winner, **AI Meta Ads Training | Sales | 02/08** (Open ad set, ₹25.1k spend, 54 purchases, ₹466 each, ROAS 2.40), was **paused while it was still winning**. Its best ad, **MTP02 "most practical"** (₹24.4k, 51 purchases, ROAS 2.29), has not run on the old setup since.
- **What would prove this wrong:** the old LP (with the same MTP02 creative) also converts under 4% when retested today. That would point to audience saturation or seasonality instead.

### Caveat on Phase A: check it against real sales first
Several Phase A campaigns report **more purchases than checkouts started**: 08 Aug (20 purchases vs 16 checkouts), 12 Aug (8 vs 7), AMTP (4 vs 2). The week of 15–21 Aug reports **41 purchases from only 17 checkouts and 216 LPVs**. That usually means the purchase event fired twice or fired on a page that isn't a real purchase. Phase A may be overstated. **Reconcile Aug 1–Sep 23 Meta purchases against Razorpay or the sales sheet before making any big decision.**

**Patterns:**
- Practical "how I actually do it" hooks (MTP02) and AI video with people on screen (AiVid 2) beat polished value-stack statics. The 5-ad Value-Stack test spent ₹1.5k for 1 purchase.
- ₹99 Protocol (Mar) and ₹199 ChatGPT Blueprint (Sep): ROAS 0.17 and 0.41. Cheap tickets bring buyers at ₹300–500 each, which only pays off with a strong upsell. Don't repeat without one.
- Many creatives were uploaded on 22 Sep and the 20-creative ABO launched at ₹2,000/day. That's about ₹100 per creative per day, well under the ₹300–500 floor each creative needs for Meta to learn. Meta coped by putting 70% of spend on one ad, which is fine. For the next ad set, use 3–4 creatives.

## 7. Structural issues

1. **Campaign-hopping:** 21 campaigns in 6 months. Most ran under 2 weeks and several ran under 3 days. Every relaunch restarts Meta's learning phase. A winner (02/08) was paused and replaced by new campaigns that never matched it.
2. **Frequency:** The Hanish ad set has lifetime frequency 8.1 and the New LP campaign 5.3. The 7-day numbers are fine for the other ad sets. The fix is fresh creative in a new ad set, not budget cuts.
3. **Tracking:** Purchases exceed checkouts in several Phase A campaigns (possible double-firing), and the APMP lead event isn't firing.
4. **Account went dark in April.** There's no evergreen always-on campaign, so every month starts cold.
